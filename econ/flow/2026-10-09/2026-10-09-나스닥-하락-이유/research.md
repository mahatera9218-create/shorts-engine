  - OpenAI 연환산 매출(run-rate) 실망 → AI·반도체 대거 매도.

  - 10년물 국채 금리 상승 → 성장주 압박.[[1]](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-08/us-stock-futures-slip-on-mounting-energy-shock-ai-bubble-fears)[[4]](https://www.reuters.com/business/wall-st-futures-slide-rising-oil-yields-dampen-mood-2026-10-08/)
