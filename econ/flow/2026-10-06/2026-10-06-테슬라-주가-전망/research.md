HSBC 등 극단적 낮은 PT.[[11]](https://finbold.com/wall-street-analyst-sets-tesla-stock-price-for-the-next-12-months/)[[8]](https://www.tipranks.com/news/tesla-stock-tsla-prediction-why-truist-is-cautious-despite-q3-deliveries-beat)
